## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-end, editorial commerce atmosphere tailored for premium Sri Lankan retail and bulk wholesale trade. Combining the stately grace of bespoke department stores with the operational rigor of a modern B2B/B2C marketplace, the visual language balances deep obsidian depths with calibrated metallic gold accents.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Style Archetype:** Modern Luxury Editorial fused with Precision Commerce. Dark mode by default, anchored in rich obsidian tones rather than flat neutral dark grays.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Multi-tiered charcoal surfaces, razor-thin borders, and selective golden reflections simulate the physical weight of polished volcanic stone trimmed with brushed brass.
- **Wholesale & Retail Dual-State:** Clear structural signifiers separate single-unit everyday luxury items from bulk wholesale pallet quantities, ensuring fluid transactions for both discerning home buyers and hospitality procurement managers.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Prestige, absolute authenticity, architectural balance, and seamless transactional trust.

## Colors

The palette leverages contrast between light-absorbing dark values and warm, reflective gold tones. Colors are deployed hierarchically to guide purchasing intent while maintaining a stately cadence.

### Core Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0B0B`):** Deep obsidian ground plane providing high visual contrast for product photography and metallic typography.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Layered depth starts at `#171717` (Surface Base) and lifts to `#1E1E1E` (Elevated Cards/Drawers), bounded by hairline edges of `#2A2A2A` or subtle gold stroke `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- **Primary Accent (`#D4AF37` - Luxury Metallic Gold):** Used strictly for core interactive commitments, wholesale threshold tags, active states, and prestige badges.
- **Secondary Accent (`#F5E6B8` - Champagne Gold):** Softened luminous tone for subheadings, price highlights (Rs./LKR amounts), and delicate interactive hovers.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - **Stock Availability / Operational Active (`#10B981`):** Muted emerald for fresh stock guarantees, wholesale availability, and safe checkouts.
  - **Critical / Tier Expiry (`#DC2626`):** Crimson gold for low inventory thresholds, flash wholesale orders, and order validation errors.
  - **Typographic Neutral High (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority titles, primary data points, and selected statuses.
  - **Typographic Neutral Muted (`#A3A3A3`):** Product provenance, secondary attributes, unit measurements, and metadata.

## Typography

The type system blends the editorial authority of high-contrast serif typography with the clinical legibility of a precision grotesque sans-serif.

### Type Pairings & Editorial Rhythm
- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for category mastheads, collection curation banners, and primary promotional headlines. Adds artisanal heritage and luxury prestige.
- **Operational UI & Tabular Numbers (`Inter`):** Assigned to SKU grids, stock statuses, packaging specifications, bulk quantity calculations, and form navigation.
- **Currency & Pricing Treatment:** 
  - The currency symbol prefix (`Rs.` or `LKR`) renders in `label-xs-caps` with uppercase tracked styling.
  - The numerical value renders in `price-lg` (`Playfair Display`) for hero catalog views, and `price-sm` (`Inter` tabular figures) for multi-tiered wholesale price matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces architectural discipline through a strict 12-column fixed-max-width grid (capped at 1440px on large viewports), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column flow on mobile viewports.

### Rhythm & Alignment
- **Horizontal Framing:** Desktop canvases employ generous outer gutters (`margin: 3rem`) creating an art-gallery matte effect against the deep `#0B0B0B` backdrop.
- **Marketplace Grid:** Product catalogs maintain an unbroken uniform rhythm. Retail showcases default to a 4-column desktop display, while complex Wholesale/Trade matrices collapse to dense 2-column or table views with reduced internal gaps to maximize density.
- **Component Padding Strategy:** Cards and inputs consistently adopt proportional vertical-to-horizontal ratios (e.g., `space-md` vertical paired with `space-lg` horizontal) to maintain an elongated, sophisticated silhouette.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurry drop shadows in favor of crisp tonal stacking, sharp architectural borders, and warm atmospheric light leaks.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Floor):** Background canvas (`#0B0B0B`), purely absorbing with no borders.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#171717` surface delineated by a 1px perimeter border of `#2A2A2A`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover Cards & Modals):** `#1E1E1E` surface accompanied by a gold hairline edge (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`) and a diffused warm ambient under-glow (`box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.05)`).
- **Level 3 (Cart Drawers, Quick View, Navigation Chrome):** Elevated charcoal glass layer using backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with `rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.85)` background fill and a continuous baseline separator of `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is tailored for luxury and structure, using understated, tailored soft corners rather than consumer rounded bubbles.

### Architectural Geometry
- **Radius Treatment:** Scaled at `roundedness: 1` (default `0.25rem` / 4px base radius, expanding to `0.5rem` / 8px on master panels and modal dialogs).
- **Visual Discipline:** The tight corner radius highlights structural integrity, mimicking bespoke packaging, gold ingots, and heavy luxury signage.
- **Micro-Elements:** Pills and full circular radii are used only for micro-counters (such as cart notification pills and tier status dots), preserving rectangular precision across primary controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury Action:** Solid Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`) fill with rich black (`#0B0B0B`) typography, font-weight 600, uppercase letter-spacing. On hover: shifts to Champagne Gold (`#F5E6B8`) with a gentle edge bloom.
- **Secondary / Trade Inquiries:** Charcoal surface (`#171717`) with a 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` and Pure White text. Hover transitions border to solid gold with ambient sheen.
- **Ghost Action:** Pure text in `#A3A3A3` with subtle gold underline animation expanding outward from center.

### Product & Wholesale Cards
- **Architecture:** Surface of `#171717` with a 1px `#2A2A2A` border. Product image nestled in a 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio on deep `#121212` background.
- **Dual-Pricing Grid:**
  - Retail: Bold price in `#FFFFFF` with Rs./LKR badge.
  - Wholesale Tier: Displays tier discount tag (e.g., "Wholesale (12+ units): Rs. 2,400/ea") enclosed in a subtle `#1E1E1E` inset box with champagne gold accents.
- **Stock Indicators:** Small 6px Emerald dot paired with `label-md` text: "In Stock (Colombo Central Hub)".

### Badges & Tier Signifiers
- **Retail vs. Wholesale Badges:**
  - *Bespoke Retail:* Minimal border badge with `#A3A3A3` border and `#F5E6B8` text.
  - *Bulk Trade Tier:* High-contrast solid badge with `#D4AF37` background, `#0B0B0B` text, and uppercase `label-xs-caps` typography.
- **Urgency / Stock Notice:** `#DC2626` background at 10% opacity, bordered by `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.4)` with solid `#DC2626` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Solid `#141414` interior with 1px border of `#2A2A2A`. Typography in `#FFFFFF`, placeholder in `#5A5A5A`. Active focus state features an explicit 1px stroke of `#D4AF37` with no heavy outline rings.
- **Bulk Quantity Steppers:** Integrated segment controller with charcoal background and hairline dividers. Decrement/Increment buttons framed in hairline borders with `#D4AF37` active markers.

### Navigation Chrome & Drawers
- **Header:** Sticky obsidian chrome (`rgba(11, 11, 11, 0.9)`) with backdrop blur, bisected by a 1px golden divider at the bottom edge. Navigation links use `label-md` uppercase with a 0.08em letter spacing.
- **Cart & Wholesale Quick-Sheet:** Slides out from the right canvas using `#171717` elevated paneling, separating line items with hairline `#2A2A2A` borders, culminating in a gold checkout breakdown.
